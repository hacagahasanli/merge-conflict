About the studio

Named after a saucer. Built for one more round.

nəlbəki · nal-BAH-kee · Azerbaijani for the saucer under a glass of tea

In Azerbaijan, tea comes in a small pear-shaped glass on a saucer, and nobody stops at one. That is the feeling we design for: a game you start in seconds and keep coming back to for another round.

Nalbaki Games is an independent studio of one developer, making original games for the browser and trying a different genre each time: a parking race, a battle royale, an office duel. All of them are free to play on a laptop or a phone.

More about the studio

Our philosophy

House rules

  1. 01A link is enough.No store, no install, no account. Seconds from the link to the first round.
  2. 02One idea, played hard.Each game is built around a single loop and tuned until round ten feels as good as round one.
  3. 03Phones are players too.Touch controls and phone layouts ship with every game, not in a later update.
  4. 04Honest numbers.Load times, build sizes and test counts are measured and written down, with sources. See the case studies.
