Parking Lot

Four cars. One free space.

Race three bots to the only empty bay in a busy car park. Touch another car, a post or a shopper and you are out. Get all four corners inside the lines, line up with the bay, and stop. First car parked wins, then the next stage opens one more floor of the car park.

One office floor. Two hits and you're out.
