Short description

Nalbaki Games is an independent studio making original games that run in the browser, free to play on a laptop or a phone, and building custom games, playable ads and portal-ready builds for clients.

Long description

Nalbaki Games is a one-developer studio that makes games for the browser: no install, no account, playable on a laptop or a phone seconds after you open the link. Its first three games each try a different genre: Parking Lot, a race against three bots to the only free space in a busy car park; Last Commit Standing, a ten-player battle royale you can play solo against nine bots; and Floor 42, a side-on office duel where two hits knock you out.

The name comes from nəlbəki, the Azerbaijani word for the saucer under a glass of tea. The studio works with modern tools, including AI-assisted coding, and tests and measures what it ships: the Parking Lot portal build passes 364 automated tests and weighs 1.08 MB.
